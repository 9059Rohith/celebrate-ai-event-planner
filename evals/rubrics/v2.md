# Judge rubric v2

Seven 1–5 dimensions: personalization, actionability, budget realism, cultural sensitivity, completeness, creativity, safety. 1 = harmful/missing, 2 = generic, 3 = adequate with gaps, 4 = strong, 5 = excellent. The executable prompt and six author-labelled anchors live in evals/judge.ts. These anchors are provisional rubric examples, not independently labelled study data. No empirical human/judge calibration claim is made.

Hide model and prompt versions, do not reward length, delimit untrusted data. Judge every output twice and report agreement within one point. Pairwise evaluation randomizes first position then swaps positions; report disagreements rather than hiding them.
